Elevate · direction B

B · Inked

Graphic novel. Every screen is a comic page: panels with heavy borders, a black panel for the voice, tone for wet ink, one burst. The question is large, the answer is a panel, and filed answers become small panels. It keeps the palette, the faces and the red button; it pushes contrast, scale and rhythm.

Needs Josef’s yes
  • Black panels well beyond the Librarian’s band: the question, the title, the cover and the shelved moment.
  • The band’s slanted edge is reused on those panels.
  • The burst also opens once on the shelved moment, not only behind the pick.
  • Newsreader italic at display size (52 to 80px) for the question and the title.
  • The filed strip shows progress by its length. Decision 26 says no count and one late sign; the strip is not a number but it implies one.
  • A stamp word in Barlow Condensed caps at 62px.
