A · Bound

Print craft. Trove is a bound book lying open on a desk: question and answer are facing pages, answers are filed on index cards, a title page is turned, the shelved date is stamped. It keeps the screentone, the three faces and the red button. It replaces the black band with paper, depth and typesetting.

Needs Josef’s yes
  • The book’s dot shadow now also sits under pages, cards, slips and the primary button, not only the book object.
  • The Librarian’s black band becomes a black page inside a white spread (still the one burst, still only the pick).
  • A ruled-paper ground made of 2px grey hairlines for the answer field. Dots stay the only shade.
