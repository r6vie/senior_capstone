Interview redesign 2 · Question and answer pages

The interview as a two-page card. The left page is black and holds the Librarian’s question in Newsreader italic, with “Skip this question” under it. The right page is white and is only the answer: a tall box, Speak, and Send. Earlier answers sit below as plain cards in a row. The question is clearly the Librarian’s and the answer clearly yours, and the only red on the screen is Send.

How it works
