Brand · round 2 · identity C

Wet Ink

A painted mark, an open book of two watercolour washes that deepen where they meet, with a Caslon wordmark tuned by eye and a type system built from book typography.

The covers are already painted with Trove’s own watercolour engine, so the brand comes from the same brush. Refined: the wordmark’s T–r gap closed and a tagline lockup added; a seven-step type scale with a job for each size; book-typography rules; and the marketing redrawn as one campaign on one grid.

Clear space

The height of the o on every side. Nothing enters it, not even a rule.

Smallest sizes

Stacked lockup 120px wide; horizontal 96px; below that the flat mark, and the word set in Caslon Text.

The tagline

Caslon italic, a sixth of the word’s size, under a hairline as wide as the word. Only on the stacked lockup.

Colour

Iron-gall ink on paper or wash. Never reversed out of a colour; on a painting, sit it on paper.
