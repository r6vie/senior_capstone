A painted mark: an open book of two watercolour washes that overlap and deepen at the spine. The site has one house mark; as a proposal, each member’s could be painted from their own cover.

The covers are already painted with Trove’s own watercolour engine, so the brand comes from the same brush. The wordmark is set in Caslon, the classic face of printed books, in blue-black ink. The mark is a living system: the site shows a house version, and each member gets their own.
