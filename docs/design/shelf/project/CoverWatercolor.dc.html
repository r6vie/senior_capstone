F · Watercolor

Sakura

Japanese watercolor and the wash of ink-and-color paintings

The idea
Wet color bleeds across the lower cover: a pastel wash with pooled, uneven edges, a dark plum branch, blossoms in layered translucent pink, a few petals drifting. A gradient mask fades everything to white by the title.
Personalized by
Wash and blossom palette (five here), which way the branch grows, and a seed that places the blossoms: no two covers are the same.
Small sizes
Below 100px the petal stamens and the paper grain drop out; the wash and the blossom masses carry the cover.
Cost
The art uses SVG filters. Render each cover once to a 244×356 image (WebP, about 15 KB) rather than filtering live on the shelf.
