Rules

The cover is the title and a first name. Nothing else. No last names, genres, counts, ratings, badges or “popular” marks. A student’s book and a VP’s book are identical objects.

White with an ink outline, a hinge line, and a dot shadow offset down and right. One shadow style for every book. Cover art is not decided yet; this white book is the placeholder for it.

A book is a link. It always has a focus ring and an accessible name: title and author.

The shelf row

Name in the display face, a rule that runs to a small “See all”, books on a ledge. Shelf names are placeholders until categories are decided. Never show how many books are on a shelf.

The feature band

The single loudest object on a screen: black, a slanted bottom edge, a burst and halo behind a tilted feature book. Use it for the Librarian’s pick. If a screen has no pick, it has no band.
