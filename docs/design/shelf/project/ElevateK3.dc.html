Kit 3 · Folio, v3

The folio, calmed down. A card is now just the card and a dot shadow under it. The shading is diagonal cross-hatching, used on the spine and under each ledge. The spine is the same hatching fading across the page. There are no stripes anywhere, including under buttons: a button just presses into its dot shadow. The cover is plain black with one book on a ledge, the shelf is books on a single ink ledge, and the dashboard tiles are gone; the one thing waiting for you is a single bookmarked line.

What changed

Depth. One layer only: the card and its dot shadow. The stacked page edges are gone.

Tabs. Low, with the open tab the same white as the page and covering its top edge, so it reads as part of the page.

Cover. Black, with Kit 2’s full burst and the book centered in it with no shadow. The frame, the stitching and the stacked edges are gone.

Shelves. One ink ledge per shelf with a light hatch below it. The dotted rule, the bookend and the dotted strip are gone.

Front page. The two dashboard tiles became one bookmarked line. “Your book” is in the top bar already.

Why it works here

One focal folio. The question and the answer are one page: the question with its large letter, an ordinary text box, Speak, Skip and one red Send.

Tabs are the phases. First look, Deeper and Check are index tabs on the folio, so there is no separate stepper to learn.

Answers so far are a plain list on a smaller folio, for rereading or fixing one. Nothing is counted.
