Kit 3 · Folio

Depth from the book as an object. Surfaces are thick folios seen from the corner: the pages show as a hatched fore-edge on the right and bottom, the spine side is shaded in dots, and sections are index tabs that stand up out of the top edge. The Librarian’s pick is a hardcover with white pages showing around the black board, and a printed frame inside the cover. It is the most literal of the three and the closest to the print direction.

How the depth works

Thickness. A folio is a block, so the page edges are drawn: hatch on the right and bottom, dot shade at the spine. Hover lifts the block, press closes it.

Tabs. Topics are index tabs. The open tab is white and joins the folio; the others sit a little lower and are paper-sunk.

The shelf. The whole shelf is one open folio with its shelves on the page, so the front page reads as one object, not a stack of boxes.
