Kit 3 · Folio, v4

The Folio kit, decided: cards are a card and a dot shadow, shade is cross-hatching, the Librarian’s cover is black with Kit 2’s burst and the book with no shadow. This version rebuilds three things. The app header now has a square search field and a square avatar that match the buttons. List rows are plain ruled rows with no markers. And the interview question is rebuilt from scratch as one composed card: the question, an ordinary answer box and a footer with Speak, Skip and Send, with the earlier answers beside it.

Needs Josef’s yes
  • Diagonal cross-hatching replaces dots for shade (the spine and under each shelf ledge). Dots stay for the cast shadow under cards.
  • The search field and the avatar are square (today the system allows a pill for both). The avatar is ink with white initials and a dot shadow.
  • Index tabs rising from the top edge, replacing the filter chips and the phase steps.
  • A ribbon bookmark (ink) on the one line that needs you. It carries no number.
  • The question’s leading letter, 68px, two lines deep (the type table stops at 22).
  • The book on the Librarian’s black cover has no dot shadow (on the black only).
  • A small corner radius (5px; 8px on the black cover; 3 to 5px on the book cover) replaces square corners on cards, buttons, fields, tabs, the avatar and the books. The fixed tier says square.

What changed

Header. The search is a square field with an icon and a “/” key hint; the avatar is a square ink tile with a dot shadow. Both use the same 2px ink line and the same 44px height as the buttons.

List rows. Plain rows with a title and a line of the answer, ruled with solid 2px lines. No markers. Hover tints the row; the selected row has a full-height bar at the left edge.

Front page. Unchanged from the last round.

The interview question, rebuilt

One composed card. The question is set big and left, with a two-line leading letter. Under it, the answer: a plain box with a label. Along the bottom, a footer strip holds everything you can do: Speak, Skip, the hint, and the one red Send.

Phases are the index tabs on the card.

Earlier answers sit in a plain card beside it, for rereading or fixing one. Nothing is counted.
