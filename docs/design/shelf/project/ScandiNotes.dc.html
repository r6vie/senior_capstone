Daylight library

A high-key room of honey birch, taken from a Scandinavian public library: ribs overhead, tall arched windows with slender slats, and slanted display units packed with face-out books. Sun through the slats stripes everything.

The vault

The header is the ceiling: diagonal birch ribs with a shallow curve. Navigation sits in it, and the current section is a lit tab.

Arched windows

Slender slats, glowing gold-white with only a trace of green outside. The Librarian's pick stands in the first window. The arch is also the shape of tabs, buttons and the avatar.

Slanted units

Each shelf is a full-width unit with slanted ends, a recessed back and a bright lip. Books face out and are packed close, six to a unit, so the page is dense and still readable.

Books, face out

A cloth color, a serif title, the first name in small capitals and one printed shape. Trove assigns color and shape, so no book is louder than another.

Schibsted Grotesk for the interface.

  • Light is the decoration. Stripes of sun through the slats cross the shelves. Nothing else glows.
  • Search is small. A field in the ceiling, never the hero.
  • No counts, genres or last names anywhere on the shelf.
  • Dense by default. No empty steps or stagger; every unit uses the full width.
