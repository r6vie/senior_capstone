The details of A, in a layout that works like a professional network. Every screen is a top bar, a main column and a side column: cards, an ordinary text box, plain lists, buttons where you expect them. What survives from A is the paper: the screentone falling from the header, the binding shade on the one focal card, the large letter on the question and back cover, the red button pressing into its dot shadow, the ink date stamp and the library card for contact details. No facing pages, no index cards, no ruled-paper text box.

  • A binding shade (a dot tone fading from the left edge) on that focal card. Still dots, no gradient.
  • A large letter (Newsreader, 76 to 118px) on the question and the back cover. The type table stops at 22.
  • The date stamp: Barlow Condensed caps at 40px, ink, once.
  • Dotted 2px rules between list rows (the rest stay solid 2px ink).
