Trove design system · v1

Foundations

Black ink on white paper, screentone dots for shade, one burst of lines for emphasis, one red for the one thing to do next.

Color

A monochrome system with one voice of color. Black ink on white paper is the whole palette; the red earns its place by being rare.

Red states

--red rest
--red-hover
--red-press

Red means “this is the one thing to do next.” One red action per screen. It is never decoration, an alert, an error or a status. Errors, success and selection use ink, bold and a dot tone, never a color.

Type

Three faces, each with one job. Caps live only in the display face and only on short labels. A sentence is never set in caps. No monospace in product UI (it is used in these boards only to name tokens).

Display · labels

Barlow Condensed

Short labels only: shelf names, nav, buttons, captions. Always caps, always tracked.

Serif · books and voice

Newsreader

What a member wrote, and what the Librarian says. The warm, literary part.

Sans · working text

IBM Plex Sans

Everything you read to do a task. Forms, details, messages, settings.

Tone and texture

Shade is made of dots, never a gradient or a blur. Tone is a ground and a shadow, never behind running text. The burst is the loudest thing the system has, so it is used once.

Tone fine.tone-fine The quiet ground behind shelves and skeletons.
Tone mid.tone-mid Scrims, empty ground, the fold of a page.
Tone dense.tone-dense The darkest dot tone, for error ground and emphasis.
Tone fade.tone-fade A tone that dissolves into paper.
Burst.burst Fanned lines. Once per screen, behind the Librarian’s pick only.
Halo.halo A dotted glow behind the feature book on the dark band.
Dot shadowThe book’s only shadow: offset dots, never a blur.
Ledge.ledge The shelf board: a solid bar with its dots falling below.

States and motion

Hover and press

HoverPressed

Hover darkens or fills. Press drops 1px and goes darkest. 120ms, plain ease. No bounce, no spring, no glow.

Focus

Focused

3px ink ring, 3px off the edge (white on the dark ground). Never removed. Visible for keyboard use only (:focus-visible).

Motion

Motion answers something the person did: a press, a hover, a page turning. Nothing animates on load, nothing loops. prefers-reduced-motion turns every transition off.
