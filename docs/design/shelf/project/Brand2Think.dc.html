Brand · round 2 · what changed

Less logo, more craft

Josef: all three were too basic, and the fonts can change to move the site away from the AI-generated look.

Why round 1 was basic
  • One idea, one flat shape.A T, a silhouette, a rectangle: an icon, not an identity.
  • Borrowed type.The wordmarks were the site's own fonts, so nothing in them was ownable.
  • No colour, no hand.Ink on paper only; nothing that could only have been made by someone.
What round 2 does
  • A made thing at the centre.A drawn bird, a two-ink print, a painted wash: each shows a hand.
  • Its own typefaces.Each direction brings a family with character, and shows the site retyped in it.
  • Colour with a reason.A sheen, two inks, or the members' own paints. Red stays only for the one action.
