The blank cover

Trove's own book object, outline, hinge and dot shadow, with the title not written yet.

The tagline turned into a picture: the book you haven't written yet. It is drawn from the screentone system we already ship, so the logo, the shelf and the covers are one family, and the dotted lines become a whole campaign (fill in the blank). Its risk is that a blank rectangle is generic, so the hinge and the dot shadow have to stay, and it must never be drawn without them.

ConstructionHow it is drawn and why
  • The book objectThe same book as on the shelf: white, a heavy ink outline, the hinge line just inside the left edge, and a dot shadow down and to the right.
  • The titleOne short bar where a title would go: written soon, not yet. It is the mark's only detail.
  • Small sizesBelow 48px the shadow goes and the hinge and title bar get heavier, because dots turn grey and a second outline reads as a copy icon.
  • The wordmarkTROVE in Barlow Condensed 800 caps, the shelf-name face, tracked +5%.
  • NeverWrite a title on the logo's cover, colour it, or swap the dots for a blur shadow.
