Overprint

A riso-printed identity in two inks. The mark is two books leaning into each other; where they overlap, a third colour appears that neither ink has alone.

That overlap is what a checkout makes: something new between two people. Grain and misregistration make it feel printed on campus; the type is Great Lakes civic signage and the American newspaper sans, not this year’s startup faces.

Two people, one sheet.Each ink is a person. Neither is on top; where they cross, the colour that appears belongs to neither of them.

Made, not rendered.Riso is the zine and campus-flyer press: grain, slight misregistration, ink that sits on the paper. The opposite of a smooth AI gradient.

Loud on a wall.The one direction that wins a crowded campus noticeboard, while the product inside stays calm.
