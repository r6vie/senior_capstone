Manga screentone

What we borrow

  • Black and white only, with shading made from screentone dots at different densities, so depth is a pattern you can count.
  • Focus lines and slanted gutters give a page energy without any colour; the panels are not on a grid.
  • One red seal (a hanko stamp) is the only spot colour and marks the action you can take.
