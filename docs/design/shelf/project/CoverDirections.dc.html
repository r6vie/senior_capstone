Cover exploration · round 1

Five cover directions

White ground for contrast, the title always at the top and in color, a band of Japanese-inspired art below. Not about what the book says: a member picks a motif and a color, so the cover feels like theirs.

LayoutTitle top (Newsreader 600, in the cover color). Art in the lower 36%. Full name at the foot. Hinge and dot shadow as in the system.
SizesShelf 122×178 is the one that matters; feature 236×344; small 92×136. Covers are vector, so they stay sharp at 2× (244×356).
LegibilityTitles up to four lines at 13.5px. Art never goes under the title. No stroke thinner than 1px at 122px wide.
PersonalizationThe member chooses motif and color. It is never generated from what the book is about. No Japanese characters on any cover.
A · Seigaiha

Waves

Ukiyo-e and kimono textile pattern

The idea
Overlapping concentric arcs, the classic wave of Hokusai’s prints and of kimono cloth. The wave field fills the lower third; the title sits above it in the same ink.
Personalized by
Color, and the size of the waves (3 to 5 rings).
Small sizes
Rings drop from 4 to 3 so each stays 1px.

B · Sumi-e

Ink wash

Brush painting in black ink, bamboo and pine

The idea
A few brush strokes and a faint wash: bamboo stalks with leaves, never the same twice. Lots of white; the title reads as the strongest ink on the page.
Personalized by
Ink color, and how many stalks and where they lean (a seed makes each unique).
Small sizes
Leaves thicken; the wash is dropped below 100px.

C · Sashiko

Stitched cloth

Boro and sashiko indigo textiles, white stitches on dyed cloth

The idea
A band of dyed cloth with running stitches. Five traditional patterns: asanoha, shippo, kikko, yabane and uroko. Strong at any size, and the closest to the screentone of the app.
Personalized by
The pattern (five to choose from) and the cloth color.
Small sizes
Stitches get longer; the pattern is never finer than 6px.

D · Kamon

Family crest

Japanese heraldry: a round mon on a plain ground

The idea
A crest in a double ring, built from a few parts: petals, wheel spokes, leaves or rings. Like a mon, it is a small mark that belongs to one person.
Personalized by
The crest (shape and count of parts) and the color. Members pick; nobody is assigned.
Small sizes
One ring is dropped; parts stay at least 3px.

E · Ukiyo-e

Fuji at dawn

Woodblock prints: flat layered color with the fade called bokashi

The idea
A sun, a cloud bank, the mountain and a water line, each a flat block of color, with a soft fade in the sky as in a hand-printed woodblock.
Personalized by
The time of day: sky color, sun position and the mountain’s tone.
Small sizes
Cloud bands and the water line merge into the mountain.
