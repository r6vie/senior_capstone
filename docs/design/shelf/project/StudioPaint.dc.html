Picks, the reveal and the design studio

Covers that make themselves as they appear

One fast timing, about 4 seconds, wherever a cover appears for you: the book reveal, your picks and the design studio. Watercolour paints itself in, glaze by glaze. Strata builds itself from the foot of the cover to the top, racing up at first and slowing as it climbs, each slab drifting down into its course, the top ones settling slowest. The slow, 25-second painting is only for watercolour on the sign-in intro (the board to the left). Each clip here holds on its finished cover for 2 seconds, then repeats.
