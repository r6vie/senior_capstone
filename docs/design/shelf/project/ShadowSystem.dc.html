Light and shadow

Everything is warm white. What separates one thing from another is how high it sits and the shadow it throws. Height always means the same thing.

The light

Left: a book in the window light throws a sharper, longer shadow. Right: the same book in room light.

Rules

  • One light, upper left. Every shadow falls down and to the right, on every screen.
  • No shadow without a reason. Sections and groups are separated by space, never by a box with a shadow.
  • Higher means longer and softer. A shadow's length tells you how far something sits off the wall.
  • Light means attention. Only the Librarian's pick stands in the window light. Nothing else glows.
  • Shadow is never the only signal. The selected book also gets an ink square, keyboard focus an ink outline, and the current tab darker text.
  • Warm shadows only. Brown, low opacity. No black, no colored glows, no blurred glass.
