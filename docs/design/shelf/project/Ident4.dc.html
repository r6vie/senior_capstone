What we borrow

  • One even line weight around everything, flat fills and no hatching, so each object reads instantly and nothing is shaded.
  • Precise, patterned architecture (bricks, roof tiles, columns) as the textures; calm blue sky as the background.
  • Shadows are a second flat tone, never a gradient. Captions are plain boxes in the corner of the scene.
